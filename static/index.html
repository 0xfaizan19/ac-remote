Siri

Say “Hey Siri, turn on the AC” or “Hey Siri, turn off the AC”. Works on the home Wi-Fi, like this app. Set it up once on each iPhone:
  1. Tap Copy “on” link below.
  2. Open the Shortcuts app, tap +, search for Get Contents of URL and tap it.
  3. Tap URL and paste the link.
  4. Tap the name at the top, rename it Turn on the AC, then tap Done.
  5. Do the same with Copy “off” link and name it Turn off the AC.
  6. Tap each new shortcut once to test it. If your iPhone asks, tap Allow or Always Allow.
